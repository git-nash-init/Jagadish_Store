Our Legacy

A Heritage of Excellence

For over six decades, Jagdish Cloth Stores has been the cornerstone of fashion and fabric in Dibrugarh. Nestled in the vibrant heart of New Market — Pucca Bazar — we have served generations of families with the finest selections in ethnic, formal, and casual wear.

What began as a humble cloth store in 1957 has blossomed into Dibrugarh's most trusted multi-floor fashion destination, offering a curated experience across four dedicated floors, each crafted to serve the unique tastes of every member of your family.
